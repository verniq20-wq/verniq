# Collecting Ho words with speakers

Verniq never invents home-language words. Its translations are only as good as the word list and phrasebook, so the most valuable work is collecting and checking words with Ho speakers (parents, elders, teachers from the community).

## Using the template
1. Open `ho-collection-template.csv` in Google Sheets or Excel.
2. It has 130 words (37 starter Ho words to **check**, 93 **missing**) and 34 everyday classroom sentences for teacher and students.
3. With a Ho speaker, fill the `target` column: correct the starter words and add the missing ones. Leave a row empty if you are unsure — empty rows are skipped.
4. Save as CSV and in the app go to **Words → Import**. Imported words become your own words and are used everywhere at once.
5. In the app, open important words and phrases and **record** the speaker saying them. Tick *Checked by a Ho speaker* for confirmed words.
6. **Export CSV** shares your list with other teachers, who import it on their devices.

## Tips
- Write Ho in Devanagari the way your community writes it, and keep spelling consistent.
- Numbers, greetings and classroom instructions help most in Class 1 — start there.
- Record in a quiet room, holding the tablet about 20 cm away; keep each clip short (under 15 seconds).
